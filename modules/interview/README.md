# Interview Coordination

**Owner:** Erica Zhao

Build the interview-coordination pipeline and its evaluation.

- **Input:** Recruiting emails, available application context, calendar availability, and prior reflections.
- **Workflow:** Interpret email, research the company/role and relevant interview experiences, prepare a brief, and propose interview times.
- **Output:** Sourced preparation material, scheduling options, confirmed calendar/reminder updates, and reusable reflection records.
- **User role:** Confirm arrangements, prepare and attend, and optionally share reflections.
- **Evaluation:** Controlled email/calendar cases; assess extraction, preparation quality, scheduling validity, and reflection persistence/reuse.

Keep module-specific skills, tools, and evaluation code here. The module can start from an email independently; deeper coaching and proactive follow-ups are optional extensions.
