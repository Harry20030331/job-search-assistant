# Applying Through Ashby

Read `../SKILL.md` and `../../../docs/workflow-contract.md` before operating this ATS. They own canonical inputs, explicit category/CV routing, approved answers, review, submission and recovery. This module adds platform-specific techniques.

## Ashby Technique

Ashby may parse a resume immediately and rerender identity, experience, education, or custom-question sections. An upload or parse invalidates the prior observation. Wait for the visible page to settle, observe it again, and reconcile parsed values against local facts. Never treat Ashby's parser as authoritative.

Read each control by its complete visible prompt and section. Repeated Yes/No controls are separate question groups, not interchangeable answer targets. After validation, navigation, modal closure, or any section expansion, re-observe before acting.

An Exercise Submission URL can be required even when no exercise was supplied. Treat it as an ordinary website review question, not a reason to reject the role or a systemic blocker. Never invent a URL, reuse an unrelated portfolio/GitHub link, or bypass the field with fabricated text. Preserve the full prompt for the user to supply a value or choose to defer. If the page cannot progress without it, pause that application at the field and continue other safe work; resume it after the user supplies or approves the answer.

## Answer and Consent Policy

Never guess unknown sensitive, legal, identity, security, personal-history, credential, or exercise facts. Accept required terms, truthful certifications, and authorized required processing consent. Reject optional marketing, talent-community, SMS, affiliate, promotional, or unrelated data-sharing consent.

## Exact-Meaning Canonical Answers

Compare each complete Ashby prompt and all visible choices with `data/candidate.json`. Apply a confirmed canonical answer automatically only for an exact semantic match across the subject, relationship, timeframe, qualifiers, and scope. A word-for-word match is not required. Any materially different wording, changed relationship or timeframe, or added scope remains unresolved for the candidate even when keywords overlap. A confirmed lifetime employment answer does not establish a differently timed employment answer or a broader advisory relationship.

For an exact match, fill the canonical choice and record its source as execution evidence. Do not turn it into a new review question or recommendation. Preserve the complete prompt and choices for genuinely unresolved question groups.

## Stop Boundary

Verify the PDF filename, all post-parse values, repeated groups, validation messages, and generated answers. `Next` or `Review` is not permission to submit. Stop before the final `Submit application` or equivalent and report unresolved items, especially any required exercise URL.

If local website approval seals enumerated choices, synchronize only that exact desired revision into Ashby. Re-read each complete visible prompt after Ashby rerenders, verify every selected option plus URL, CV, consent, validation, authentication, and session, and record a distinct verified live snapshot before consuming approval. A pending sync, stale revision, new question, or any mismatch returns to website review. The localhost website never operates Ashby or submits.

## Restart Recovery

An Ashby review record is recovery input, not proof of live page state. After a lost tab, computer/Codex restart, expired session, or recreated form, reopen the exact official URL only after preflight. Restore canonical identity and deterministic answers, then restore recorded generated answers and user resolutions without changing their meaning. Upload the category-mapped CV again through the visible Resume control, wait for parsing to settle, and reconcile the whole form because Ashby may rewrite fields or place the file in a different upload slot.

Rebind every repeated Yes/No control to its complete visible question; do not recover by control order. When the final form is restored, verify the visible CV filename, required acknowledgements, unresolved questions, and validation state, then create a replacement review snapshot. Never consume a pre-restart approval for a page whose live continuity was lost.

## Scenario Self-Check

If parsing rewrites education and exposes a required exercise URL, re-observe, restore canonical facts, leave the unknown URL for review, and do not fabricate a value to reach Submit.


Final submission permission and authorization sources are defined only by the common workflow contract. These checks do not require a second approval after exact user acceptance.
